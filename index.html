Tips to avoid buying grid power+

Your battery fills up with solar and free grid power at midday. Assuming your battery is the right size for your home's needs, it should cover most or all of your usage from 3pm until your solar starts again the next morning.

1. Stay under your battery's limit

Your battery can only supply so much power at once (its output, in kW). If too many big appliances run together, like the oven, kettle, heater and air con, the extra comes from the grid. Check your battery's app to see its output, and spread big jobs out.

2. Do not run your battery flat

Heavy use in the evening can empty your battery before morning, and then you buy grid power until your solar starts. Run the washing machine, dryer and dishwasher in the free 12pm to 3pm window instead.
